# P5 -> Vectors

Two eyes follow the mouse. Uses `p5.Vector` (`createVector`, `mag`, `setMag`,
`p5.Vector.add`) to limit the pupil movement to the eyeball. Helper lines and
points visualise the vectors involved.

Further reading: [Nature of Code – Vectors](https://natureofcode.com/vectors/),
[p5.Vector reference](https://p5js.org/reference/p5/p5.Vector/)
